Contact

Trail tips, lunch stops, corrections and questions are all welcome.

The best tips on this site will come from riders who've already been there. If you know a trail, a stop or a piece of gear that deserves a closer look, or you caught me getting something wrong, I want to hear about it.

Email

hello@pedalpause.com

I read everything myself. This is a one-person site, so give me a few days to reply.

Have an event?

Use the event submission form. It asks for the date, the place and a link to the organizer so I can confirm the details before I list it.

Found a mistake?

Tell me what's wrong and, if you can, where you found the right answer. Corrections get fixed and noted on the page, with the date. Trail closures, new rules and restaurants that shut down are exactly the kind of thing I want to know about.

Companies and PR

Happy to hear about products that could make riding easier for people over 55. Read how this site handles free gear first. The short version: nobody gets to approve a review.
